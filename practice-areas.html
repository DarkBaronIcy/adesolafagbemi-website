OUR EXPERTISE

Legal Solutions Across Diverse Practice Areas

With over a decade of experience, Adesola Fagbemi & Co provides practical legal solutions for individuals, businesses, multinational corporations and government institutions across a broad range of legal disciplines.

01

Corporate Commercial Law

We advise companies, entrepreneurs and investors on corporate governance, regulatory compliance, business formation, mergers and acquisitions, commercial transactions, contract drafting and corporate restructuring.

02

Commercial Real Estate

Our firm provides legal services relating to land acquisition, property transactions, title perfection, leases, mortgages, commercial developments and commercial land recovery.

03

Shipping & Admiralty

We represent clients in maritime transactions, shipping disputes, vessel documentation, cargo claims, marine insurance matters and other admiralty-related legal issues.

04

Oil & Gas

We advise stakeholders across the upstream, midstream and downstream sectors on regulatory compliance, commercial agreements, project development and legal risk management.

05

Asset Recovery

We assist clients in tracing, protecting and recovering assets through strategic legal action, negotiation and enforcement proceedings.

06

Trusts & Wills

We provide estate planning services including wills, trusts, probate administration, succession planning and wealth preservation for families and businesses.

07

Dispute Resolution

We represent clients in litigation, arbitration, mediation and other forms of alternative dispute resolution, delivering practical strategies aimed at protecting our clients' interests.
